# Security policy

Do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities, exposed credentials, or
content/privacy concerns. Contact the maintainers privately through the security
contact published in the repository settings, including a clear reproduction and
impact assessment. Do not include secrets, user data, or private corpus content.

The public demo is not a production service. Deployers must provision their own
Cloudflare resources, secrets, rate limits, and monitoring before serving users.
